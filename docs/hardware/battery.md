## Use

Put a battery in a battery bay of a [case](/hardware/case/): tier 1 has one bay, tier 2 two, and
tier 3 four. A computer is ready to run when it has a [CPU](/hardware/cpu/) and a battery with
charge. The case view shows the charge, the load, and how long the batteries will last.

A battery is rated in days of a running computer: a [CPU](/hardware/cpu/)'s 100 W and a
[screen](/hardware/screen/)'s 10 W, 110 W. By default:

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lasts, full | 10 days | 20 days | Never runs out |
| Recharges itself | no | yes, while it is not in use | not needed |

These lengths can be changed in the game's settings. A tier 2 battery recharges at half the rate
of a running computer's draw, so two seconds of rest charge one second of running.

A new battery is empty. Charging and power are shown in watt-hours; see [Power](/concepts/power/).

## Crafting

- **Tier 1:** a row of three copper ingots, then iron, redstone, iron, then iron, redstone, iron.
- **Tier 2:** a tier 1 battery in the middle (it keeps its charge), a row of three gold ingots above
  it, iron ingots left and right of it, and below it a sea lantern, a blaze rod and a sea lantern.
- **Tier 3:** a tier 2 battery in the middle (it keeps its charge), diamonds in the top corners with a
  nether star between them, netherite ingots left and right of the middle and in the bottom
  corners, and an eye of ender below the middle.

## Charging

Put a battery and a glowing item in the crafting grid, in any arrangement. Each item adds a share of
a full tier 1 battery (a tier 2 holds twice as much, so the same item fills half as much of it):

| Item | Adds |
|---|---|
| Sea lantern | 100% |
| Glowstone | 25% |
| Shroomlight | 25% |
| Glow ink sac | 10% |
| Glow lichen | 5% |

A full battery takes nothing. A tier 3 never needs charging.

A robotic piston is a piston a computer can push with.

## Use

Place a robotic piston and join it to a computer: a [cable](/hardware/cable/) goes on its one cable
port, the face it was placed against. It can push on every other face, when a program says so. See
[Robotic Piston](/devices/robotic-piston/).

It moves blocks as a piston does: the blocks in front move (the game's own piston rules, so it
cannot push obsidian or too many blocks), its arm comes out, and goes back after a moment. A push
costs power whether or not it moves anything. A program can also hold an arm out until it retracts it.

The sticky one pulls back what is in front of its arm when the arm goes back.

Instead of a cable, a [Bluetooth card](/devices/bluetooth-card/) in its slot links it to its computer
(right-click the piston to open the slot).

## Crafting

- **Robotic Piston:** a chip in the middle, pistons above, left and right of it, a copper ingot below
  it, and iron ingots in the four corners.
- **Robotic Sticky Piston:** a slime ball above a robotic piston.

## Notes

- The arm is a block of its own, and cannot be pushed.

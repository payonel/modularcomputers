## Use

Put sticks in the RAM sockets of a [case](/hardware/case/): a tier 1 case has one, tier 2 two, and
tier 3 four. A computer's memory is the RAM installed, above a measured baseline, so a stick means
the same to a program whatever the firmware itself costs. Tier 1 RAM holds mcos to its shell.

Higher tiers also add ring memory: room for the pieces of device data the computer keeps for the
program, for each device. Tier 1 adds none, tier 2 adds 2 and tier 3 adds 4. See
[Interrupts](/concepts/interrupts/).

## Crafting

- **Tier 1:** a row of three chips, then copper nugget, iron ingot, copper nugget, then a row of three
  gold nuggets.
- **Tier 2:** a tier 1 RAM in the middle, a row of three chips above, gold ingots left and right of
  it, and a row of three gold nuggets below.
- **Tier 3:** a tier 2 RAM in the middle, a row of three chips above, diamonds left and right of it,
  and a row of three gold nuggets below.

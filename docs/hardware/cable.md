## Use

Place cables between a computer and the screens, hubs and other devices you want on its
[bus](/concepts/bus/). A cable reaches out to every neighbor that takes one on that side: other
cables, and screens, computers, hubs and the other cabled blocks on any side but their front. Its
model shows each connection it makes.

A block right against a computer, not against its front, is on its bus without a cable.
[Screens](/hardware/screen/) and [expansion hubs](/hardware/expansion-hub/) pass the connection on;
other devices end it. A [case](/hardware/case/) ends it too: a cable can join two computers, but
a computer is not a switch.

A device is on one computer's bus at a time. The first computer to have it holds it.

You can also join devices without a cable using a [Bluetooth card](/devices/bluetooth-card/).

## Crafting

A copper ingot in the middle with dried kelp in the eight places around it makes 8 cables.

## Notes

- A cabled block can close the port on a side, so no cable joins there.
- External devices are hot-plugged: one that is not connected is not on the bus.

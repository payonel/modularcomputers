The All-in-One Computer is the creative tab's computer: a block that works as a tier 3
[case](/hardware/case/), with no recipe.

<div class="infobox" markdown="1">

### Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| Kind | Block |
| Tier | 3 (the slots of a [tier 3 case](/hardware/case/)) |
| Stack | 64 |
| Recipe | None: find it in the creative tab |

</div>

## Use

It works as a tier 3 case does: open the case view from the window on its back, fill the slots, and
press the power button. As in any tier 2 or 3 case, a [screen](/hardware/screen/) in its gold
expansion slot shows on its front; right-click the front to use the screen and keyboard.

In survival, craft a [tier 3 case](/hardware/case/) instead.

## Notes

- A running all-in-one (its [CPU](/hardware/cpu/)'s 100 W and a screen's 10 W) is the yardstick for
  [batteries](/hardware/battery/): they are rated in days of one running.

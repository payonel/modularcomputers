A Modular Computers computer is built from parts, the way a real one is. You craft a case, set a
CPU, some RAM, an EEPROM, a battery and a disk into its slots, add cards for what you want it to
do, and give it a screen. Then you press its power button.

## Building one

1. Craft the [chips](/hardware/photomask/) every part is made from.
2. Craft a [case](/hardware/case/) and place it. It faces you.
3. Open the case from its back and fill the slots: a [CPU](/hardware/cpu/), an
   [EEPROM](/hardware/eeprom/), a [battery](/hardware/battery/), [RAM](/hardware/memory/) and a
   [disk](/hardware/disk/).
4. Give it a [screen](/hardware/screen/): in a tier 2 or 3 case's gold slot, or beside it, joined
   by [cable](/hardware/cable/).
5. Press the power button on its back.

The CPU decides how many devices the computer's [bus](/concepts/bus/) holds, so a bigger CPU lets
you wire up more. Devices outside the case (screens, hubs, a Disk Station) join the bus by cable,
or by Bluetooth.

## Pages

- [Case](/hardware/case/): tiers 1 to 3, the block everything goes in.
- [All-in-One Computer](/hardware/all-in-one/): a creative tier 3 computer with its screen built in.
- [Broken Computer](/hardware/broken-case/): decoration found in the world.
- [Screen](/hardware/screen/): tiers 1 to 3, a display and keyboard.
- [CPU](/hardware/cpu/): sets the bus size and how much power the computer draws.
- [RAM](/hardware/memory/): sets how much memory programs get.
- [EEPROM](/hardware/eeprom/): holds the program a CPU starts.
- [Hard Disk](/hardware/disk/): tiers 1 to 3, storage, with two formats.
- [Battery](/hardware/battery/): powers a computer.
- [Cable](/hardware/cable/): joins a computer to the devices outside it.
- [Expansion Hub](/hardware/expansion-hub/): card slots on the cable network.
- [Disk Station](/hardware/disk-station/): formats, wipes, clones and installs disks.
- [Photomask](/hardware/photomask/): prints chips from silicon.
- [Robotic Piston](/hardware/robotic-piston/): a piston a computer can push with.
- [Bot Golem](/hardware/bot-golem/): a robot a block high, a computer that walks.
- [Charging Pad](/hardware/charging-pad/): charges a robot standing on it.

## Cards

Cards go in a case's expansion slots, or in an [expansion hub](/hardware/expansion-hub/). Each has
its own page in the Devices section, with what a program can do with it:

- [Redstone Card](/devices/redstone-card/)
- [Network Card](/devices/network-card/) and [WLAN Cards](/devices/wlan-card/)
- [Bluetooth Card](/devices/bluetooth-card/)
- [Sound Card](/devices/sound-card/)
- [Internet Card](/devices/internet-card/)
- [Operator Card](/devices/operator-card/) (creative only)
- [Motion Sensor](/devices/motion-sensor/), [Audio Sensor](/devices/audio-sensor/) and
  [Weather Sensor](/devices/weather-sensor/) cards

Every part has a tooltip: hold Shift over it to see how it works and what its crafting does.
